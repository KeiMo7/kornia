Multi-view camera calibration in `kornia.geometry.calibration`: `calibrate_camera` fits an OpenCV Brown-Conrady pinhole camera and `calibrate_fisheye` a Kannala-Brandt fisheye camera from per-view world-to-pixel correspondences, and `calibrate_cameras` calibrates several cameras independently with serial or parallel execution. The pipeline runs RANSAC homographies for planar boards (with a DLT-PnP fallback for non-planar targets), a staged Levenberg-Marquardt bundle adjustment with Schur pose elimination, deterministic point and view rejection, and distortion-order reduction. Results are returned as a `CalibrationResult` bound to a camera id, image size and model tier, with per-view diagnostics and re-projection errors recomputed through the public projection functions; unsatisfiable observations return `success=False` with named reasons rather than raising. The existing projection, undistortion and PnP functions are unchanged.
